## [a568b73] - Added calculator

You may ask, how is it different from [this](./learn/calc/calc.c).

The old calculator I made earlier didn't care about operator precedence or parentheses. It simply parsed operators from left to right.

This one is different!

It correctly handles operator precedence and supports parentheses. I've also tried my best to make the code easy to understand with comments explaining how the parser works.
